How the videos are made

Episodes are produced with a small, private software tool run by the channel's operator on their own computer. Scripts are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools and checked against the cited sources. The narration voice is AI-generated (open-source text-to-speech). Music and sound effects come from free libraries and are credited in each description.

The same tool uploads the finished videos to the channel's own YouTube and Instagram accounts using the official YouTube Data API and Instagram API. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign in to it, and it does not access anyone else's accounts. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
